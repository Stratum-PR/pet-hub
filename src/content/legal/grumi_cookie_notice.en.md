## 2. What cookies are and how you can manage them

2.1 A **cookie** is a small file placed on your computer or mobile device by your browser when you visit a website. It helps the site remember information between pages or visits (for example, sign-in state, language, or your consent choices). Similar technologies (such as local storage) may be used for equivalent purposes.

2.2 Most browsers let you refuse or delete cookies and alert you when a new cookie is set. See your browser’s help section for details. The following links explain cookie controls for common browsers:

- [Google Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647)
- [Microsoft Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge/delete-cookies-in-microsoft-edge-63947406-40ac-c3b8-57b9-2a946a29ae09)
- [Apple Safari](https://support.apple.com/guide/safari/sfri11471/mac)
- [Mozilla Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/cookies-information-websites-store-on-your-computer)
- [Opera](https://help.opera.com/en/latest/web-preferences/#cookies)

2.3 On the Site and Platform you can manage non-essential cookie categories through the banner shown on your first visit (until you save a choice) and at any time via **Cookie settings** in the footer.
